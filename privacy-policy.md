# Privacy Policy

This service sends automated SMS alerts to a single authorized user (the owner of this system). No personal data is collected, stored, shared, or sold to any third parties.

The only information used is the mobile phone number that receives alerts. This number is manually configured by the owner and is not collected from external users.

No user accounts, cookies, analytics, or tracking technologies are used.

If you wish to stop receiving messages, reply STOP at any time.
